# Lesson Plan — Week 16: CryptoVault studio

**02005104 · Cryptography and Network Security · Year 4 · Semester 2 AY2026 · Lecture**

**Contact time: 100 minutes.**

Teams of 3 normally, 2 where uneven. Grade through the existing 15% project rubric; no added studio category. The ≤5-minute video is submitted before Week 19. Remaining clarification uses Week 20; do not reserve Q&A inside the Week 19 practical. Every team demonstrates its existing sharing flow to its peer-review partner. During the work block, the instructor schedules short checks with teams against the five required controls; this is not a full presentation to the whole class. Existing practice practical challenges ([W17 mock bank](../../labs/week17-review/mock-ctf.md)) check understanding before the final assessment and add no marks.

## In-class run sheet

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Administer the scored Week 15 retrieval quiz after its lesson, then give the studio brief. |
| 10–20 | Teams check the five primitives, trust boundaries and outstanding tests. Prioritize controls for wrong passwords, tampered ciphertext, substituted sharing keys, altered signatures and invalid TLS certificates. |
| 20–75 | Implement/test the current build; instructor conducts focused scheduled clarification during team work; existing practice practical challenges are available to check understanding. |
| 75–95 | Peer review: each team demonstrates its sharing flow to a partner team; update the self-audit from peer and instructor checks. |
| 95–100 | Save remaining fixes, contribution notes and the final-deliverable plan. |
